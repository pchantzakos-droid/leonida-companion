Privacy Policy — Leonida Companion

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Leonida Companion (“the app”) is published by quietcodeworks (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version: the app collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. Everything you type stays on your phone.

1. What we collect

Nothing.

We do not collect, transmit, store on our servers, sell, or share any personal data. We operate no servers and have no way to see anything you enter.

The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting SDK, no advertising network, no social login, no tracking pixels and no user accounts.

2. What you enter, and where it lives

Everything you create in the app — tracker items, cheats, vehicles, shopping goals, income sources, play sessions, and your settings — is written to a private database inside the app’s own storage on your device.

That storage is sandboxed by Android: other apps cannot read it, and neither can we. The data is removed when you uninstall the app, or when you use Settings → Delete all data inside it.

Backups you create yourself

Pro users can export a backup to a single JSON file. The app writes that file only to the exact location you pick in your device’s file picker. Once the file exists, it is an ordinary file on your device or in whatever storage provider you chose, and it is governed by that provider’s terms rather than by this policy. We never see it.

Android system backup

The app allows Android’s standard backup and device-transfer feature, which is a function of your device and your Google account, not of this app. If you have that feature switched on, Android may copy the app’s data to your Google account so it can be restored to a new phone. That transfer is handled entirely by Google and is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can disable it in your Android settings. We have no access to those backups.

3. Purchases

Leonida Companion offers one optional, one-time in-app purchase (“Pro unlock”). Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing.

We never see, receive or store your payment details, card number, billing address or Google account. The app asks Google Play whether this device’s Google account owns the Pro product, and stores only a single yes/no flag locally so that Pro keeps working when you are offline.

Google’s handling of your purchase is covered by the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

The app requests no storage permission. Backup export and import use Android’s Storage Access Framework, where you choose the file and the system grants access to that one file only.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

6. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or erase on request. You are in full control of your own data: export it with the backup feature, or erase it with Settings → Delete all data or by uninstalling the app.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new “last updated” date. Because the app collects nothing, we do not expect material changes.
